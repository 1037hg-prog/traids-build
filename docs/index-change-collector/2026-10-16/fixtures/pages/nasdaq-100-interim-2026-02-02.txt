Nasdaq-100 Index Constituent Change

Announcement Date: February 2, 2026

Nu Example Logistics Inc. (Nasdaq: NUEX) will replace Psi Example Media Group (Nasdaq: PSIM) in the
Nasdaq-100 Index effective prior to market open on Friday, February 6, 2026. Psi Example Media Group is
being acquired by Tau Example Group and will no longer be eligible for the index.

A further change has been determined and is announced today; its effective date depends on a regulatory
approval that has not yet been granted.

The following securities will be removed from the Nasdaq-100 Index effective on a date to be announced:
