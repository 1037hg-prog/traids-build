S&P Dow Jones Indices Announces Change to the Effective Date of an Index Change

NEW YORK, September 10, 2026: S&P Dow Jones Indices announced today that the addition of Zeta Example
Corp to the S&P 500, announced on September 4, 2026, will now take place effective prior to the open of
trading on Tuesday, September 29, 2026. The pending acquisition of Lambda Example Industries is now
expected to close on Monday, September 28, 2026.

Effective Date  Index Name  Action    Company Name         Ticker  GICS Sector
09/29/2026      S&P 500     Addition  Zeta Example Corp    ZETA    Information Technology
09/29/2026      S&P 500     Deletion  Lambda Example Ind.  LMEX    Industrials
