Annual Reconstitution of the Nasdaq-100 Index

NEW YORK, Dec. 12, 2025 -- Nasdaq today announced the results of the annual reconstitution of the
Nasdaq-100 Index, which will become effective prior to market open on Monday, December 22, 2025.

The following securities will be added to the Nasdaq-100 Index:

Company Name                     Ticker
Alpha Example Holdings Inc.      ALEX
Upsilon Example Software Ltd.    UPSX
Chi Example Biosciences Inc.     CHIX

The following securities will be removed from the Nasdaq-100 Index:

Company Name                     Ticker
Beta Example Industries Inc.     BEXI
Rho Example Resources Corp.      RHOX
Nu Example Logistics Inc.        NUEX

The Nasdaq-100 Index is composed of 100 of the largest non-financial companies listed on The Nasdaq
Stock Market based on market capitalisation. The annual reconstitution is based on market values as of
the last trading day in November.
