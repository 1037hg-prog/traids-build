S&P Dow Jones Indices Announces Changes to the S&P 500, S&P MidCap 400 and S&P SmallCap 600 Indices

NEW YORK, September 4, 2026: S&P Dow Jones Indices ("S&P DJI") will make the following changes to the
S&P 500, S&P MidCap 400 and S&P SmallCap 600 indices effective prior to the open of trading on Tuesday,
September 22, 2026:

Effective Date  Index Name          Action    Company Name            Ticker  GICS Sector
09/22/2026      S&P 500             Addition  Zeta Example Corp       ZETA    Information Technology
09/22/2026      S&P 500             Deletion  Lambda Example Ind.     LMEX    Industrials
09/22/2026      S&P 500             Deletion  Omicron Example Co      OMIC    Industrials
09/22/2026      S&P MidCap 400      Addition  Omicron Example Co      OMIC    Industrials
09/22/2026      S&P MidCap 400      Deletion  Kappa Example Holdings  KAPX    Health Care
09/22/2026      S&P SmallCap 600    Addition  Kappa Example Holdings  KAPX    Health Care
09/22/2026      S&P 500 ESG Index   Addition  Theta Example Inc       THET    Utilities
09/22/2026      S&P Composite 1500  Addition  Zeta Example Corp       ZETA    Information Technology

Zeta Example Corp (NYSE: ZETA) will replace Lambda Example Industries (NYSE: LMEX) in the S&P 500
effective prior to the open of trading on Tuesday, September 22, 2026. Lambda Example Industries is
being acquired by a private investor group in a transaction expected to close on or about September 18,
2026. Omicron Example Co and Kappa Example Holdings are each moving down one index, and are more
representative of the mid-cap and small-cap market spaces respectively.
