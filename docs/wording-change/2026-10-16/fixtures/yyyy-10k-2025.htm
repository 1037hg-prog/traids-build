PART I

Item 1. Business

Sample Gadgets Corp makes gadgets. We sell them through two channels: a wholesale channel that serves builders & fitters, and a retail channel that serves the public. Our products are assembled from parts bought from more than forty suppliers.

We hold no patent that we consider material to the business as a whole.

Item 1A. Risk Factors

Gadget demand follows construction activity. A downturn in building would reduce the number of gadgets sold through the wholesale channel, which is the larger of our two channels, and we would not be able to move that volume to the retail channel.

We depend on a single logistics provider for deliveries to the wholesale channel. If that provider failed, deliveries would be late until another could be engaged.

Item 3. Legal Proceedings

We are a defendant in an ordinary course dispute with a former supplier. We do not expect the outcome to be material to the business, the financial position or the results of operations.

Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations

Revenue was higher than in the prior year. The wholesale channel grew and the retail channel was flat. Margin was unchanged because the cost of parts rose at about the rate that prices did.

We ended the year with cash on hand and no borrowings. We expect operations to fund the coming year.
